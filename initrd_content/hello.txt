Dobro pozhalovat' v MyOS!
===========================

Eto prostaya operatsionnaya sistema, napisannaya s nulya.
Ona podderzhivaet:
  - Zashchishchyonnyj rezhim (Protected Mode)
  - Preryvaniia (IDT, ISR, IRQ)
  - Klaviaturu PS/2
  - Tajmer PIT
  - Upravlenie pamyat'yu (PMM, Paging, Heap)
  - Fajlovuyu sistemu InitRD
  - Virtual File System (VFS)

Versiya: 0.9
Avtor: MyOS Team
