O sisteme MyOS
===============

Nazvanie:    MyOS
Versiya:     0.9
Arhitektura: x86 (i386, Protected Mode)
Yadro:       Monolitnoe
Pamyat':     Do 512 MB (bitmap PMM + paging)
FS:          InitRD (nachal'nyj RAM-disk)
Klaviatura:  PS/2 (IRQ1)
Tajmer:      PIT 8254 (100 Hz)
Video:       VGA Text Mode 80x25, 16 tsvetov
